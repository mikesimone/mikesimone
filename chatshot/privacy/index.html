Chatshot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

The short version: Chatshot does its work entirely on your device. Your screenshots are never uploaded to us, and we don't have any account system, server, or database that stores your information. The only outside service involved is Google's ad network, which shows the banner and occasional ads and may use your device's advertising ID to do so.

Who we are

Chatshot ("the app") is published by GCBApps. You can reach us at mike@mikesimone.net. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

What Chatshot does with your screenshots

Chatshot lets you pick screenshots from your device, trims and condenses them into a single image or animation, and lets you share the result. All of this — reading the images, on-device text recognition, editing, anonymizing, and rendering — happens locally on your device. Your screenshots and the images you create are never sent to us or to any server. When you tap Share, your image is handed to whichever app you choose through Android's standard share sheet; where it goes from there is up to you and that app.

Information we collect

We (GCBApps) do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Chatshot has no login, no user accounts, and no analytics of our own. We do not have a server that receives your data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chatshot is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use certain information, most notably your device's advertising ID and related device/usage signals, to serve and measure ads and to help prevent fraud. This data is handled by Google, not by us.

You can learn how Google uses this information, and your choices, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices). Buying the "Remove ads" option (below) also stops ads from being shown.

Purchases (Remove ads)

Chatshot offers a one-time Remove ads purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see or store your payment card details. Whether you own this item is tracked by Google Play against your Google account so ads can be turned off — we do not keep our own record of it.

Permissions

Chatshot uses network access only so the ad network can load ads. It reads the images you explicitly pick through Android's photo picker, and it does not access any other files.

Children

Chatshot is intended for users 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the date at the top.
